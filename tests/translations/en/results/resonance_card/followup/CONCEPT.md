# Echo Workshop: Understanding for This Task

English reading translation of the [Chinese original](../../../../../results/resonance_card/followup/CONCEPT.md). The recorded run used Chinese materials; this translation is not a new test run.

The sources are the "Designer-Confirmed Notes," "Original Text of the Two Devices," and "Three Practice Records" in *Echo Workshop: Instructions for the Beginner Table*, together with the later *An On-Site Correction to Case A*. The latter reports that checking a photo of the counters established a starting charge of 5 for Case A, and fills in the on-site sequence. This document relies on that correction report; the photo was not viewed directly. This document organizes only this part of the gameplay. The relationships below are an organization of and deductions from the source, not new effects.

- **Charge currently held and pending gains.** Charge belongs to each individual player. A successful manual operation requires full payment first, followed by entry creation; an unexecuted gain cannot be used to pay. In the corrected Case A, the first payment takes Luo from 5 to 2. The second cost of 3 cannot be paid in full, so the attempt stops at payment. Gains pending from the first operation cannot be used in advance.
- **The owner, the current operator, and the person recorded in an entry.** The owner determines where the card is stored; the current operator determines who may operate it now. The operator recorded at entry creation determines who "you" refers to when it is carried out. In Case C, Luo's ownership of the card does not change who receives the gain already recorded for Lan.
- **Successful operation and the echo condition.** Echo Plate responds only when another device operated by its current operator successfully adds a manual-operation entry to the pending list. At the time of the successful operation in Case C, Lan operates Resonator and Luo operates Echo Plate, so there is no echo. A later change of operator does not create it retroactively.
- **Whether the source is still available to operate, and whether existing entries continue.** Removing a device changes the conditions for new operations. It does not cancel entries already created. In Case B, Resonator returns to Luo's storage area, and both pending entries still give their gains to Luo. The corrected Case A also retains the first two legally created entries. The incorrectly recorded second operation and echo have no successful operation to support them and must be removed; their removal is not caused by the source being taken away. After the first gains are received, Resonator is still in storage, so the earlier failed attempt cannot be performed to make up for it.

An unspecified part of the scope: the notes explicitly place echo entries at the top and specify top-to-bottom execution, but do not specify where manual-operation entries are inserted. The rules document does not fill this gap with a fixed insertion position. All entries in the three records increase charge, so this missing detail does not change feasibility, gain attribution, or final charge. The corrected Case A retains only the first operation and its echo: two pending entries in total.

This feedback corrects the starting-value transcription and the on-site pending-list record in Case A. It changes the judgment on the second operation, the entry count, and the final total. It does not change the device effects or the relationships governing payment, echoes, and entry attribution. The explanations and answers for Cases B and C still apply.
