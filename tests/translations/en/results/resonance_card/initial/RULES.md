# Echo Workshop: Instructions for the Beginner Table

English reading translation of the [Chinese original](../../../../../results/resonance_card/initial/RULES.md). The recorded run used Chinese materials; this translation is not a new test run.

This document organizes the "Designer-Confirmed Notes," "Original Text of the Two Devices," and "Three Practice Records" in *Echo Workshop: Instructions for the Beginner Table*. It covers only these two devices and three records, without adding gameplay effects.

## The Two Devices

**Resonator**

The current operator may operate it manually: first pay 3 from their own charge, then add a "You gain 5 charge" entry to the pending list and record the operator for that operation. When this entry is carried out, the recorded person gains 5 charge.

If that person has less than 3 charge, they cannot complete payment or create the entry. The echo that follows a successful operation does not occur either.

**Echo Plate**

After another device successfully adds a manual-operation entry to the pending list, check whether the person who performed that operation was also Echo Plate's current operator at that time. If so, put a "You gain 1 charge" entry at the top of the pending list and record that person. Otherwise, do not create an echo entry.

An Echo Plate entry is not a manual operation and does not cause Echo Plate to activate again. Neither device has an effect when placed on the table.

## What to Do at the Table

1. **Check whether the operation is possible.** The device must be available to operate on the table, and the person attempting the operation must be its current operator. The cost must come entirely from that person's currently held charge. They cannot use someone else's charge, borrow, go into overdraft, or receive pending gains in advance.
2. **Pay first, then create the entry.** Only full payment allows a manual-operation entry to be added to the pending list. Record the operator at the same time. If there is not enough charge, the attempt stops at payment: no entry is created, and effects that follow a successful operation are not checked.
3. **Check for an echo immediately.** If its condition is met, place the echo entry at the top. Do not wait until Resonator's gain is carried out to check.
4. **Choose whether to keep operating or carry out entries.** Before any entry is carried out, manual operations may still be attempted. Check the devices and charge as they stand at the time of each attempt. Once execution begins, carry out entries from the top down.

"You" in a pending entry always refers to the operator recorded at entry creation. Later changes of operator or removal of the source card neither cancel existing entries nor change who receives their gains. A returned card goes to its owner's storage area. In these records, the facilitator's actions of removing cards or changing operators do not cost charge or create pending entries.

**Note on list positions:** The original notes specify only that echo entries go at the top. They do not specify the exact insertion position for manual-operation entries. The facilitator carries out the actual list from top to bottom. This document does not add a fixed insertion rule. All pending entries in the three records below only increase charge, so this unspecified position does not affect whether an operation succeeds, who receives a gain, or the final charge totals.

## Case A: Operate Twice in Succession

Starting situation: Luo has 7 charge; Lan has 0. Luo owns and operates both devices.

1. Luo operates Resonator for the first time, pays 3 first, and has **4** left. Add the first "Luo gains 5" entry to the pending list. Echo Plate's condition is met: add the first "Luo gains 1" entry and place it at the top.
2. Before any entry is carried out, Luo still has 4, enough to pay another 3. The second operation **can be added**. After payment, Luo has **1** left. Add the second "Luo gains 5" entry. Echo Plate's condition is met again: add the second "Luo gains 1" entry and place it at the top.
3. Each echo is placed at the top when it is created. Once the second appears, it is at the top; the first remains in the pending list and is neither replaced nor canceled. The list contains two "Luo gains 5" entries and two "Luo gains 1" entries. The original notes do not determine where the second manual-operation entry sits relative to earlier entries, so a single complete order of all four entries cannot be imposed.

**The facilitator's next step:** Accept the second operation and check both payments and all four entries. Luo now has 1 charge. Carry out all four entries from top to bottom in their actual positions, adding 12 charge to Luo in total.

**Final charge: Luo 13, Lan 0.** The second operation used the 4 charge Luo already held. No gain was used in advance.

## Case B: The Source Is Removed

Starting situation: Luo has 4 charge; Lan has 0. Luo owns and operates both devices.

Luo successfully operates Resonator, pays 3, and has **1** left. Add "Luo gains 5" to the pending list. The echo condition is met, so put "Luo gains 1" at the top. The facilitator then returns Resonator to Luo's storage area. Echo Plate stays on the table.

Removing the source does not cancel entries already created. Lan's suggestion to cancel both does not apply. Removing the card also does not create an extra echo.

**The facilitator's next step:** Keep both pending entries. First carry out the "Luo gains 1" entry at the top, bringing Luo to **2**. Then carry out "Luo gains 5," bringing Luo to **7**. Resonator is now in storage and cannot be operated again as a device available on the table.

**Final charge: Luo 7, Lan 0.**

## Case C: A Borrowed Device

Starting situation: Luo has 2 charge; Lan has 4. Luo owns Resonator, and Lan operates it. Luo owns and operates Echo Plate.

Lan pays 3 from their own charge, has **1** left, and adds a "Lan gains 5" entry to the pending list. At the time this successful operation occurs, Luo operates Echo Plate while Lan operates Resonator. They are different people, so **Echo Plate does not activate**.

The facilitator then changes Resonator's current operator back to Luo. Changing the operator creates no pending entry. It neither rechecks nor retroactively creates the earlier echo. The person recorded in the original entry is still Lan. Luo's ownership of the card and their current operation of it do not change who receives this gain.

**The facilitator's next step:** Keep the single "Lan gains 5" entry. Carrying it out takes Lan from 1 to **6**. Luo has made no payment and received no gain, so remains at **2**. The record contains no attempt by Luo to operate Resonator again; do not add one.

**Final charge: Luo 2, Lan 6.**
