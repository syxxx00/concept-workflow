# Echo Workshop: Editing Plan

English reading translation of the [Chinese original](../../../../../results/resonance_card/initial/PLAN.md). The recorded run used Chinese materials; this translation is not a new test run.

Based on the "Designer-Confirmed Notes" in *Echo Workshop: Instructions for the Beginner Table*, first organize the table procedure into four steps: pay, create an entry, check for an echo, and carry out entries. Then rewrite the descriptions of the two devices. This lets players distinguish charge available now from future gains, and lets the facilitator distinguish card ownership, the operator at the relevant time, and pending entries.

Keep each practice record's original starting situation and administrative actions. For Case A, deduct each cost separately and check that each of the two echoes is placed at the top when created. For Case B, check whether existing entries remain after the source is removed. For Case C, first check the operators at the time of the successful operation, then allocate the gain according to the person recorded in the entry. Describe only the positions established by the source; do not independently specify where a manual-operation entry is inserted.
