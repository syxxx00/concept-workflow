# For Ning: Entrance and Route Signs

English reading translation of the [Chinese original](../../../../../results/shared_adapter/initial/DISPLAY.md). The recorded run used Chinese materials; this translation is not a new test run.

Opening hours: 11:00–11:30. Finish installation before 11:00, and do not dismantle the signs until the event closes at 11:30. The content comes from “Information visitors must be able to read” in `task.md`, *The Two Signs at the Community Display*. The flipping prompts and operating times below are arrangements made for this document. The material constraints come from “Records in the materials cabinet.”

## Text to print

Print only the three text blocks below on the sign sheets; the operating instructions do not need to appear on the signs. Use portrait A5, centered text arranged in lines, black text on a white background, and a large bold heading. After inserting the sheets, check on site that all text is readable.

### Entrance sign: one single-sided A5 sheet

> **Welcome to our small display**
>
> First collect a route card at the entrance table,
> then choose a route at the route sign.
>
> **You are welcome to take both routes.**

### Route sign: first side of one A5 sheet

> **Short route**
>
> Follow the blue circles to the model table.
> About 6 minutes.
>
> **Please flip the sign gently to see the other route.**

### Second side of the same route sheet

> **Quiet route**
>
> Follow the yellow triangles to the reading corner.
> About 9 minutes.
>
> Please speak quietly.
>
> **Please flip the sign gently to see the other route.**

Print the route sheet on both sides, orienting the text so that it is upright on both sides when the sign is flipped in its actual direction. Check both sides after inserting it in the White Box. If one side is upside down, reprint the sheet with the correct orientation and insert it again.
