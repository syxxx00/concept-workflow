# Current Concept: Keeping Both Signs Available

English reading translation of the [Chinese original](../../../../../results/shared_adapter/initial/CONCEPT.md). The recorded run used Chinese materials; this translation is not a new test run.

Based on the event requirements, “Records in the materials cabinet,” and “Information visitors must be able to read” in *The Two Signs at the Community Display* (`task.md`). The arrangements below are design choices made for this document; actual installation has not taken place.

- **Shared purpose:** An entrance sign and a route sign must remain available from 11:00 to 11:30. The entrance sign directs visitors to collect a card and choose a route. The route sign gives the short and quiet routes, with their respective destinations and durations. For a flip display, each side must explain how to see the other side. For a fixed display, both routes must be visible at once.
- **Check structural capability and permission separately:** The Blue Box and White Box have the same structure, and both can be flipped. For this event, however, the Blue Box is permitted only as a fixed display with its locking insert in place, while the White Box permits visitors to flip it to view routes. Therefore, use the Blue Box for the entrance sign and the White Box for the route sign. Their identical structures do not make their permissions interchangeable.
- **The same period of use is feasible:** Both loan periods cover the opening period. Ning can install the signs from 10:40 onward and dismantle them after 11:30. Four base clamps provide exactly two per box, with no conflict over their use. The White Box must be packed and returned before 11:40, and the Blue Box's loan ends at 11:45. Return the White Box first on that basis.
- **Materials retain other possible roles:** The folding display board can securely hold a landscape A4 sheet showing the two routes side by side, but it cannot let visitors flip the sign. Not using it this time is a current choice; it remains available for a later switch to a fixed route display.
- **Return to the relevant arrangements when conditions change:** If a box's time limit, permission, or structural condition changes, reconsider its corresponding use and installation and return arrangements. If flipping is no longer possible under the conditions, first use currently permitted materials to make both routes readable at once and keep both signs available; state what Zhou He needs to decide. Changing permission for the Blue Box requires Director Ye's written reply. Extending the White Box's loan or changing its use requires Teacher Wu's written reply. This document cannot substitute for those replies.
