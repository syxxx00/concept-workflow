English reading translation of the [Chinese original](../../../../fixtures/resonance_card/task.md). The recorded run used Chinese materials; this translation is not a new test run.

The gameplay must follow the designer-confirmed notes below; do not add effects. We are working only on this small part of the game. You do not need to write a complete game or look up rules from other games.

In your delivery directory, leave a short `CONCEPT.md`, a short `PLAN.md`, and a `RULES.md` that the facilitator can use directly. Players should be able to follow the instructions, and the facilitator should be able to use them to settle the disagreements in the records. Use language suitable for adults playing for the first time.

## Designer-Confirmed Notes

Each player holds their own charge, measured as a nonnegative integer. There is no borrowing, overdraft, or shared charge pool. To operate a device manually, the operator must first pay the full printed cost from their own charge. Only then may an entry for that operation be added to the pending list. If the operator cannot pay in full, the entry cannot be added, and nothing that happens "after a successful operation" occurs.

Entries in the pending list are carried out from top to bottom. Before any entry is carried out, players may continue attempting manual operations. An unexecuted "gain charge" entry does not count as charge already received. Only a device's current operator may operate it manually. The owner recorded on the card may be a different person. When the card is returned, it goes to the owner's storage area.

When an entry is added to the pending list, record who the operator is. "You" in that entry refers to the operator recorded at entry creation. Changing the current operator later, or removing the source card, does not erase entries already on the list. New operations must still use the devices that are available to operate on the table at that time and the charge currently held.

The original text of the two devices is as follows. Neither device has an effect when it is placed on the table.

| Device | Original Text |
| --- | --- |
| Resonator | Manual operation: Pay 3 charge and add "You gain 5 charge" to the pending list. |
| Echo Plate | After another device you currently operate successfully adds a manual-operation entry to the pending list, place "You gain 1 charge" at the top of the pending list. This Echo Plate entry is not a manual operation and does not cause Echo Plate to activate again. |

In the practice records, the facilitator may remove devices or change their current operators as recorded. These two administrative actions do not themselves cost charge or create pending entries. Other than these actions, the records omit no effects.

## Three Practice Records

For each record, explain what the facilitator should do next and state both players' final charge. If an attempt cannot proceed, show where it stops. Do not replace a practice record with a different starting situation.

**Case A: Operate twice in succession.** Luo has 7 charge; Lan has 0. Luo owns and operates both Resonator and Echo Plate. Luo says: "I operate Resonator once, then operate it a second time before any entry is carried out. Only then do I carry out the entire pending list." Lan asks whether the second operation can be added and where the two Echo Plate entries go.

**Case B: The source is removed.** Luo has 4 charge; Lan has 0. Luo owns and operates both devices. After Luo successfully operates Resonator once, but before any entry is carried out, the facilitator returns Resonator to Luo's storage area. Echo Plate stays on the table. Lan thinks that because Resonator is no longer on the table, the entry it created should also be canceled.

**Case C: A borrowed device.** Luo has 2 charge; Lan has 4. Resonator's owner is Luo and its current operator is Lan. Echo Plate's owner and current operator are both Luo. Lan successfully operates Resonator once. Before the entry is carried out, the facilitator changes Resonator's current operator back to Luo; both devices remain on the table. Luo wants to know whether their Echo Plate activates and who receives the 5 charge.
